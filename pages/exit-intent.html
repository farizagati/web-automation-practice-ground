Exit Intent

Move your mouse out of the viewport (towards the browser address bar) to trigger an exit intent modal.

ℹ️ Move your mouse to the top of the page (outside the viewport) to trigger the exit intent popup.

How Exit Intent Works

Exit intent technology tracks mouse movement and detects when the user is about to leave the page. It's commonly used for:

  • Last-minute discount offers
  • Newsletter subscription prompts
  • Preventing cart abandonment
  • Survey requests

Testing Tips

// Simulate exit intent by moving mouse out of viewport
from selenium.webdriver import ActionChains

actions = ActionChains(driver)
# Move mouse to coordinates outside viewport
actions.move_by_offset(0, -100).perform()

// Or trigger via JavaScript
driver.execute_script("""
    document.dispatchEvent(new MouseEvent('mouseout', {
        clientY: -10
    }));
""")
